# Wearable Privacy Policy Archive

This repository provides archived privacy policy documents and evidence materials used in an academic study on informed consent in wearable smart devices.

The materials are provided to support evidence verification, methodological transparency, and peer-review assessment. The final study sample consists of 18 device-document cases involving wearable smart devices that collect health-related or physiological data.

## Repository Contents

- `01_Evidence_Matrix_Chinese_Complete.xlsx`  
  Complete Chinese evidence matrix based on the original Chinese policy texts.

- `02_Evidence_Matrix_English_Auditable.xlsx`  
  Complete English evidence matrix for peer-review and editorial assessment.

- `03_Evidence_Matrix_Chinese_Readable_Short.xlsx`  
  Shorter Chinese evidence matrix for internal checking and readability.

- `04_Document_Selection_Flow_Diagram.docx`  
  Document selection flow diagram showing how the final 18 samples were identified.

- `05_Cohens_Kappa_Assessment_English.docx`  
  English explanation of the Cohen's kappa assessment.

- `06_Cohens_Kappa_Assessment_Chinese.docx`  
  Chinese explanation of the Cohen's kappa assessment.

- `07_Privacy_Policy_Archive_AG01_AG18.zip`  
  Archived privacy policy and user-agreement documents for the final 18 samples.
