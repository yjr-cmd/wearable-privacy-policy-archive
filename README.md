# Wearable Privacy Policy Archive

This repository supports editorial and scholarly verification of the documentary analysis reported in the manuscript *Safeguarding Informed Consent in the Collection of Personal Health Data by Wearable Smart Devices: An Empirical Study of User Agreements from Chinese Devices*.

## Repository contents

1. `01_Evidence_Matrix_Chinese_Complete.xlsx` — complete Chinese evidence matrix.
2. `02_Evidence_Matrix_English_Auditable.xlsx` — auditable English evidence matrix, including the two coders' pre-consensus classifications, final codes, evidentiary passages, coding reasons, 252 case-level applicability reasons, and the case inventory.
3. `03_Evidence_Matrix_Chinese_Readable_Short.xlsx` — readable Chinese evidence matrix.
4. `04_Document_Selection_Flow_Diagram.docx` — document-selection flow diagram.
5. `05_Cohens_Kappa_Assessment_English.docx` — English inter-rater-reliability assessment report.
6. `06_Cohens_Kappa_Assessment_Chinese.docx` — Chinese inter-rater-reliability assessment report.
7. `07_Privacy_Policy_Archive_AG01_AG18.zip` — dated text snapshots and original/source files for AG01–AG18, with a manifest mapping each case to its source and archived files.
8. `08_Coding_Manual.docx` — coding definitions, decision rules, and aggregation rules.
9. `09_Device_and_Document_Applicability_Inventory.docx` — per-case device, application, policy owner, document title, third-party list, date, source, archive, cross-device scope, independence rationale, and D1/D2 applicability audit.

The Cohen's kappa DOCX files are assessment reports rather than standalone calculation workbooks. The reported statistics can be independently recalculated from the complete pre-consensus `Coder 1` and `Coder 2` columns in the auditable evidence matrix.

## Scope and versioning

The analysis uses the archived document versions identified in the evidence matrix and case inventory. Live links are included as provenance, but the fixed snapshots in the archive are the materials used for verification. Online policies can change, and later versions fall outside the analysed snapshot.

The final matrix contains 252 coded cells (18 device–document cases × 14 indicators): 158 fully covered (`✓`), 62 partially covered (`○`), and 32 absent (`x`). These values are descriptive summaries of documentary coverage. They do not establish the companies' actual data-processing practices, interface behaviour, user comprehension, voluntariness, or the ethical validity of consent in individual cases.
